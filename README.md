Tobb Technologies – Business Growth & Marketing Campaign

Campaign Title

Your Business. Better Connected.

Project Overview

This project was prepared as part of the WIL Business Growth & Marketing Challenge. It presents a proposed digital marketing campaign for Tobb Technologies.

The campaign aims to increase awareness of the company and encourage small businesses and entrepreneurs to explore its digital solutions.

Target Audience

* Small-business owners
* Entrepreneurs
* Start-ups
* Business decision-makers in Johannesburg

Campaign Materials

The repository contains:

* Marketing campaign proposal
* Promotional graphic
* Social media captions
* Customer outreach email
* Evidence folder for project screenshots

Campaign Message

Your Business. Better Connected.

The campaign focuses on helping businesses explore digital solutions that can support their operations and growth.

Connection to Work-Integrated Learning

During my WIL experience, I gained exposure to software development and the reengineering of an existing web application called Zonke. This project allowed me to apply my understanding of technology to a business and marketing-related task.
