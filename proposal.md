Tobb Technologies Business Growth & Marketing Campaign Proposal

Campaign Title: Your Business. Better Connected.

Prepared for: Tobb Technologies
Project: Business Growth & Marketing Challenge
Date: 30 October 2026

1. Introduction

Marketing plays an important role in helping businesses reach customers, communicate their services and build awareness. As more businesses use digital platforms, companies need effective ways to promote their solutions online.

This proposal introduces a digital marketing campaign for Tobb Technologies called Your Business. Better Connected. The campaign is designed to improve awareness of the company and encourage small businesses and entrepreneurs to explore its digital solutions.

2. Company Background

Tobb Technologies is the company connected to my Work-Integrated Learning experience. Its contact details and official website are included in this proposal.

During my WIL experience, I gained exposure to software development and the reengineering of an existing web application called Zonke. This helped me understand how technology contributes to business operations and the importance of maintaining and improving digital platforms.

3. Marketing Opportunity

Small businesses increasingly depend on technology to communicate with customers and manage their operations. However, some businesses may not know where to find suitable digital solutions.

A digital marketing campaign can help Tobb Technologies introduce its services to a wider audience. Social media and online promotional content can make it easier for potential customers to discover the company.

4. Campaign Objectives

The proposed campaign aims to:

* Increase awareness of Tobb Technologies.
* Reach small-business owners and entrepreneurs.
* Encourage potential customers to visit the company website.
* Generate interest in the company’s digital solutions.
* Build a consistent and professional online presence.

5. Target Market

The campaign will focus on:

* Small-business owners.
* Entrepreneurs and start-ups.
* Business managers and decision-makers.
* Organisations looking for digital and technology solutions.

The initial geographical focus will be Johannesburg and surrounding areas.

6. Campaign Strategy

The campaign will use digital marketing methods to reach the target audience.

6.1 Social Media Marketing

Social media platforms such as Facebook, LinkedIn and Instagram can be used to share promotional messages, company information and updates.

6.2 Promotional Graphic

A promotional graphic has been prepared using the campaign message Your Business. Better Connected. The design is intended to attract attention and communicate the campaign theme.

6.3 Website Promotion

The company’s website, https://tobb.io can be included in promotional content so that interested customers can find more information.

6.4 Customer Outreach

A draft outreach email has been prepared to introduce Tobb Technologies to potential business customers and encourage enquiries.

7. Campaign Message

Your Business. Better Connected.

The message communicates the idea that technology can help businesses improve how they operate and connect with customers.

8. Marketing Materials

The following materials have been prepared:

1. A promotional graphic.
2. Social media captions.
3. A customer outreach email.
4. A written marketing campaign proposal.

These materials are included in the repository.

9. Implementation Plan

The proposed campaign can be implemented in the following stages:

Stage 1: Preparation

Review the promotional content and confirm that the company information and services are accurate.

Stage 2: Content Approval

Allow Tobb Technologies to review and approve the campaign materials before publication.

Stage 3: Campaign Launch

Share approved content on suitable social media platforms and include the company’s website.

Stage 4: Monitoring

Monitor engagement, website visits and customer enquiries.

Stage 5: Evaluation

Review the campaign results and identify areas that could be improved.

10. Resources Required

The campaign may require:

* A computer or smartphone.
* Internet access.
* Social media accounts.
* Promotional graphic and written content.
* Access to the company website.
* A person responsible for monitoring enquiries.

11. Measuring Campaign Performance

The campaign’s performance can be measured using:

* Social media reach and engagement.
* Likes, comments and shares.
* Website visits.
* Customer enquiries.
* Responses to outreach messages.

These measurements can help determine whether the campaign is reaching its intended audience.

12. Possible Challenges

Some possible challenges include limited marketing resources, low engagement and competition from other technology companies.

These challenges can be addressed by publishing relevant content, using suitable platforms and reviewing campaign performance regularly.

13. Conclusion

The Your Business. Better Connected. campaign provides a proposed approach for promoting Tobb Technologies through digital marketing.

Preparing this campaign helped me connect my WIL experience in technology and software development with the business side of the IT industry. It also helped me understand how digital communication and marketing can support business growth.
